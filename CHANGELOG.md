Completed the first India-first pre-product research cycle for Kids Learning OS.

- expanded evidence/source registry;
- consolidated Product Foundation v1.0 readiness report.

### Major research conclusions

- “All-in-one study app” is rejected as a sufficient product thesis.
- The strongest moat hypothesis is a persistent Child Learning Model / learning-intelligence layer.
- Independent Mastery is the leading candidate learning north-star.
- Ages 6–8 / Grades 1–2 is the leading first commercial wedge hypothesis, subject to direct validation.
- Ages 4–5 should receive a distinct play/off-screen/parent-oriented experience rather than a smaller version of a school tutor.
- India architecture must assume Android, shared family devices and imperfect connectivity.
- AI should use a hybrid local/deterministic/cloud architecture rather than always-on premium inference.
- Child mode must remain ad-free, non-manipulative, finite and parent-controlled.

### Phase transition

Broad desk research is no longer the critical path. The project is ready to move into **Product Definition + Direct User Validation**.

Future ages 9–12 and 13–16 research remains important for long-term expansion but is not required before validating the first foundational-stage wedge.
