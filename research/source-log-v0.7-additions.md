- **URL:** https://www.pratham.org/about/teaching-at-the-right-level/
- **Level:** A/B
- **Used for:** Teaching by actual learning level rather than grade alone.

### Teaching at the Right Level randomized evaluations
- **Source:** NBER
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w22746
- **Level:** A
- **Used for:** Evidence that targeted instruction can improve learning when grade-level curricula exceed actual learner level.

### NIPUN Bharat / Foundational Learning Study
- **Source:** Ministry of Education / NCERT
- **URL:** https://nipunbharat.education.gov.in/fls/fls.aspx
- **Level:** A
- **Used for:** Granular foundational literacy/numeracy competencies and one-to-one assessment structure.

### Randomized trial of AI tutoring
- **Source:** Scientific Reports / PubMed record
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40537565/
- **Level:** A
- **Used for:** Evidence that carefully designed AI tutoring can improve learning in an older-student context; explicitly not generalized directly to ages 4–8.

### OECD/PISA — AI use, reading and learning
